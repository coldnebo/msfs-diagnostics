Signs: pitch + nose up, bank + right wing down, rotation + clockwise seen from above (right). Rotation is about the vertical axis (body rates projected), which counts spin turns correctly near vertical. In a flat spin the indicated airspeed is meaningless. Vertical lines mark detected events: W warning, A yoke full aft, P peak AoA, D departure, R pro-spin rudder, Y yoke forward, O opposite rudder, S rotation stopped, L level.
